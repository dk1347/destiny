# Destiny — Korea MVP Demand Hypotheses

> Research date: 2026-09-16
> Status: Hypothesis and experiment plan, not a market-size claim

## 1. Decision framing

The initial Korea MVP needs a small, testable acquisition funnel. It must not
claim that a topic has the highest demand or conversion merely because an AI
report, app listing, review, or search snippet says so.

The current working funnel is:

```text
free daily reading or compatibility entry point
        ↓
relationship-focused follow-up exploration
        ↓
paid comprehensive Saju result
```

This is a product hypothesis. It becomes a product decision only after an
experiment measures its own acquisition, completion, and purchase behavior.

## 3. Findings that remain unconfirmed

The following claims from the earlier exploratory report remain hypotheses
until their original datasets and methods are checked:

- A numeric TOP 10 ranking of Korea fortune topics.
- The relative search volume of daily fortune, compatibility, romance, reunion,
  comprehensive Saju, or wealth readings.
- Content-view totals and category shares from any third-party platform.
- The claim that relationship content converts better than comprehensive Saju.
- Any market-size, MAU, or demographic percentage without a primary source.

These claims must not be used in investor, marketing, or product-performance
statements at this stage.

## 4. MVP experiments

### Experiment A — Entry-point interest

Compare two equally prominent entry paths for eligible users:

- A: daily reading
- B: compatibility or relationship reading

Measure impression-to-start rate, completion rate, and voluntary return within
seven days. Do not infer purchase intent from a click alone.

### Experiment B — Follow-up relevance

After a completed free result, offer one clearly labeled follow-up relevant to
the result context. Compare a relationship-focused follow-up with a
comprehensive Saju follow-up. Measure opt-in rate and completion rate; keep
the same price and disclosure conditions if a paid test is later approved.

### Experiment C — Paid proposition

Only after free-flow usability is stable, compare a comprehensive paid result
against a narrow, transparent topic result. Measure payment conversion,
refund/contact rate, and repeat use. Never make deterministic claims about
relationships, health, law, or finance.

## 5. Evidence needed before changing the funnel

An experiment or external market finding can change the MVP funnel only when
it includes:

1. Population, country, collection date, and methodology.
2. A directly inspectable source or retained raw/aggregate data.
3. A pre-defined metric and comparison group.
4. Known limitations, including selection bias and seasonality.
5. A human product-owner decision record.
