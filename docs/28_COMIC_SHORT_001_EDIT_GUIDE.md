# Destiny — Comic Short 001 Edit Guide

> Status: internal edit brief. Do not publish without the approvals listed in
> `docs/27_COMIC_SHORT_001_VARIANT_TEST.md`.

## Master setup

- Canvas: 9:16 vertical, 1080 × 1920 px.
- Runtime: 21 seconds.
- Source: use either paired storyboard asset from `docs/27` as the visual
  master; crop each of its five horizontal panels into a separate shot.
- Audio: optional neutral, licensed instrumental only. The edit must work with
  audio muted because every factual sentence is captioned on screen.
- Do not add animated fortune symbols, currency, clocks, or outcome imagery.

## Caption layout

- Use a high-contrast charcoal text color on an off-white rounded caption
  plate. Do not rely on the illustration background alone for readability.
- Keep captions inside the central safe area: 120 px from each side, at least
  250 px above the bottom, and at least 220 px below the top.
- Main caption: maximum two lines, large enough to read on a 6-inch phone at
  arm's length. Supporting voice captions may be smaller but must not compete
  with the main caption.
- Keep `출생시간`, `연주`, `월주`, `일주`, `시주`, and `절기 전환일` exactly
  as written; do not replace them with predictive or mystical wording.

## Paired-variant invariants

For the male/female comparison, change only the storyboard asset. The crop,
timing, captions, caption positions, audio, transitions, title, CTA, and
publication metadata must remain the same.
